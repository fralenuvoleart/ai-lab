## News Coverage Preferences

### Core Requirements
- Geographically balanced worldwide coverage — not US/UK-centric
- Stories should represent every inhabited continent
- Must include smaller/underreported regions (e.g., Caucasus, Central Asia, Balkans, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America)

### Methodology
A region-first approach targeting local outlets per region rather than relying solely on international aggregators.

### Regions
- Western Countries
- Caucasus / Central Asia
- Southeast Asia
- South Asia
- Latin America
- Middle East
- East Asia

### Priority Countries
- Italy
- Georgia

### Specialist Topic: Hacker & Cybersecurity
A dedicated section covering cybercrime, AI, privacy, and digital rights, sourced from: Hacker News, The Record, BleepingComputer, Ars Technica and the infosec communities.

### What to Avoid
- Defaulting to BBC/CNN/Reuters/AP-only aggregation
- Missing granular local stories that don't make mainstream trends
- Treating "world news" as "what US/UK outlets report"
