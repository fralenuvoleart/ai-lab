## News Coverage Preferences

### Core Requirements
- **Geographically balanced worldwide coverage** — not US/UK-centric
- Stories should represent every inhabited continent
- Must include smaller/underreported regions (e.g., Caucasus, Central Asia, Balkans, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America)

### Methodology
When gathering news, use a **region-first approach** targeting local outlets per region rather than relying solely on international aggregators:

#### Source Strategy by Region
- **Caucasus/Central Asia:** OC Media, Civil.ge, Agenda.ge, Eurasianet
- **Africa:** Al Jazeera Africa, BBC Africa, local outlets
- **Southeast Asia:** Al Jazeera Asia-Pacific, regional outlets
- **South Asia:** Al Jazeera, BBC, Indian/Pakistani outlets
- **Latin America:** BBC Latin America, regional outlets
- **Middle East:** Al Jazeera, BBC Middle East
- **Europe (beyond UK):** Al Jazeera Europe, local outlets per country
- **East Asia:** Al Jazeera Asia-Pacific, BBC Asia

### What To Avoid
- Defaulting to CNN/Reuters/AP-only aggregation
- Missing granular local stories that don't make international wires
- Treating "world news" as "what US/UK outlets report"

### User Expectation
If a story like "Thai tourist found dead in Tbilisi" exists, it should be caught. If it's missed, explain why (tool failure, robots.txt, etc.) and improve methodology.

### Priority Countries
- **Italy:** Essential news must always be included. Sources: Il Post, ANSA, La Repubblica, Ansa.it (English), local outlets.
- **Georgia (country):** Essential news must always be included. Sources: OC Media, Civil.ge, Agenda.ge, Georgian Journal, local outlets.

### Specialist Topic: Hacker & Cybersecurity World
Must always include a dedicated section covering:
- Major hacks, breaches, data leaks, ransomware attacks
- Cybersecurity policy and regulation changes
- Hacker culture, collectives, and notable arrests/indictments
- Vulnerability disclosures, exploit news, zero-days
- Dark web / cybercrime developments
- Privacy, encryption, and digital rights
- Sources: The Record (Recorded Future), BleepingComputer, KrebsOnSecurity, Ars Technica, The Hacker News, infosec Twitter/Mastodon
