# Georgian American University (GAU)
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/georgian-american-university-gau.jpg)
Since 2005, the Georgian American University (GAU) in Tbilisi, focuses on international standards of education.
Since 2005, the Georgian American University (GAU) serves to establish international standards of education, develop competitive professionals and successful individuals. GAU is located in Tbilisi, Georgia.
The University has a student-oriented approach with an increasing role for interdependent study.
Students’ rights and interests are protected completely in university, friendly and ultra-modern international atmosphere are oriented to guarantee high-level educational processes.
