## Opening a Bank Account in Georgia: Quick Overview
Non-residents can open personal and business bank accounts in Georgia either in person or remotely via a Power of Attorney (POA). While compliance and due diligence checks have tightened, major Georgian banks offer robust multi-currency capabilities and multi-lingual online banking apps. Working with local representatives helps minimize risk and streamlines the verification process.
  * **Remote Timeline:** 2 to 4 weeks total processing time from document preparation to full digital access.
  * **Supported Currencies:** Standard multi-currency accounts operate in **GEL, USD, and EUR**.
  * **Remote Requirements:** A notarized and apostilled Power of Attorney (POA) along with a certified copy of the applicant’s passport.
  * **In-Person Timeframe:** Roughly 30 minutes for a standard walk-in application at a local branch (subject to a subsequent 1-day KYC compliance review).
  * **KYC Requirements:** Applicants must declare their source of wealth, source of income, and expected account turnover (proof of local residential address or utility bills is generally not mandatory).
  * **Top Banking Choices:** Bank of Georgia (BOG) / Solo Bank, TBC Bank, Liberty Bank, and Terabank.

## Who can open a bank account in Georgia?
In Georgia, not only citizens, but also foreigners can use banking services. But in recent years, Georgian banks have tightened requirements for them. Due diligence is more thorough. There are a number of sanctioned countries that are denied opening an account by default. These countries include Syria, Iran, Afghanistan, North Korea and other countries that are under international sanctions. For citizens of Russia and Belarus, refusals, of course, occur, but rarely. Residents of other countries get accepted almost all the times. With the correct submission of all the necessary documents, an undesirable verdict of the Bank can be avoided.
If a foreign citizen has a [residence permit in Georgia](https://pbservices.ge/services/visa-residency-georgia/), then this greatly simplifies the opening of an account, and also increases the credibility of the bank. If for the first time a refusal is received from the bank, you should not despair. It is necessary to apply to several financial institutions and even branches, with a high degree of probability one of them will agree to open an account.
## Types of accounts in Georgian banks
  * **Settlement account** – with its help you can make transfers around the world without restrictions, exchange currency, pay bills. You can make transactions and deposit funds in three currencies: GEL, USD and EUR. Entrepreneurs and legal entities open a current account in a bank of Georgia to conduct business.
  * **Deposit account** – savings, the owner of such an account receives profit as a percentage of the amount on the balance sheet. You can withdraw the received funds every month or at the end of the contract, depending on the conditions of a particular bank.
  * **Card account** – is a familiar plastic card that non-residents need for comfortable payments and purchases.

## Requirements for opening a bank account
Foreign citizens can personally visit the bank branch and open account with a not very complicated procedures, which takes around 30 minutes. If this is not possible, you can issue a power of attorney for a representative who will go through this procedure on your behalf. The power of attorney and a photocopy of the passport must be notarized.
Foreign citizens who have lived in Georgia for 183 days become tax residents, which significantly increases the chances of being approved by a banking institution.
## Step-by-step procedure to open a bank account **remotely**
### Find a proxy who will go to the bank on behalf of you
There’s only one way to open a bank account without visiting the bank. And that way is**to issue a power of attorney to someone who will visit bank on your behalf**. The power of attorney is the document in which you give all the rights to represent you in front of the bank. This document needs to be **notarized** (and in some cases also **apostilled**) and sent to Georgia in the paper form. No digital power of attorney are acceptable by the banks unfortunately. On top of this, banks will require from you the notarized and apostilled **copy of your passport**.
**The power of attorney** must be drafted in a way that is acceptable in all the banks in Georgia. There are certain rules for how the power of attorney text should be drafted. For example, if you give the power to a proxy to just open a bank account then the proxy will be able to open a bank account but he or she will not be able get access to the newly opened account. Banks will not give the usernames and other keys to him/her. Subsequently he/she will not be able to give those usernames to you. And in this case, you will need to go through the process of notarizing the Power of Attorney all over again. There aren’t quite a number of these details that should be taking to taking into consideration before notarizing the power of attorney.
You should also consider **date when the power attorney is expired**. The date is very important. you must choose the date between the one that is not too long so that you don’t give your proxy too long time for managing your account and at the same time it shouldn’t be too short. The date must be just enough to open a bank account.
### Notarize and apostille the document
After the **text of the power of attorney is drafted** the important step is to notarize and apostille the document. In different countries different state organization put an apostille on a notarized document, but the golden rule is to approach a local notary, to ask to notarize the power of attorney (the text that was already drafted) and then ask the notary to assist in the apostille process.
In certain countries and in certain situations the apostille and notarization process is complicated. However, there are number of online notary solutions where you can notarize an apostle your power of attorney without leaving your house. The process takes about 30 minutes. It is very efficient way to start the process of bank account opening in Georgia. One of the most reliable and efficient online notarization service providers that we work with are at Scribbe Seal. They provide
  * U.S. Online Notarization Worldwide
  * U.S. Apostille Service

Any documents including POA and the copy of the passport notarized and apostilled by Scribble Seal is accepted by the banks in Georgia.
Another mandatory document to submit to the bank before opening bank account is notarized and apostilled copy of the first page of the passport. This document can also be notarized and apostilled the same way we notarize the power of attorney.
### Send the documents to Georgia to your representative in Georgia
Once an certification of the documents is finished you will need to **send the documents to Georgia** to your proxy. Using the fast post like DHL FedEx and UPS is the best way because documents will not be lost and will be delivered in time. As we mentioned above the time is very important for opening a bank account. We need to put the date of the expiration of the power attorney just enough to open a bank account, not more not less.
If you used the online notary to notarize your documents, they will send the documents directly to Georgia. You don’t have to bother yourself with organizing the Courier services.
### Translate and notarize the document and KYC procedures
Once the documents arrived to Georgia the foreign language documents needs to be translated into Georgia and the translation needs to be notarized by the local notary. This will take about one or two days.
Before submitting to the bank a list of documents required to open a bank account, uou will need to go through the Know Your Customer (KYC) procedure.
In the process of the KYC you need to disclose three different numbers to the bank
  * Source of your wealth
  * Source of your income
  * Expected turnover on the newly opened bank account

Please note these are numbers that you need to indicate but banks in most cases will not ask you for the proof of any of the numbers that you indicate. Moreover Georgian banks are among a very few banks worldwide who do not require proof of you address till now. So they just ask you where your address is they will not ask you to prove that by utility bill, lease contract or anything similar.
If the bank approved the questionnaire (assigned a low risk level to the non-resident. And in the most cases if you come from the non sanctioned country you will be assigned a lower risk level and will not be denied to open a bank account) you move to the next stage to actually open an account.
### Submit documents for the account opening
**The exact list to submit of documents in most banks are** :
  * notarized and translated into Georgian power of attorney
  * notarized in translating it to Georgian copy of the passport
  * filled KYC questionnaire

**However, depending on the requirements of a particular bank the list of document may increase:**
  * Confirmation of the legality of the origin of income, for example, an employment contract or a real estate lease agreement;
  * Documents confirming the ownership of real estate, assets and other property;
  * Bank statement for the last six months;
  * Information about the place of residence with supporting documents: lease agreement, utility bills. For some banks, information about accommodation in a hotel or registration address in the country in which the client is a citizen is sufficient;
  * For citizens of America – IRS (an account in the tax service that complies with US legislation on the taxation of foreign accounts);

When opening an account, a Georgian bank usually issues a debit card and access to Internet banking to the client.
Within 1 working day, the bank will check the documents. There’s nothing left to do but wait. If the verification was successful, then your proxy will receive a notification from the bank in which you will be invited to receive account details, otherwise you will receive a refusal message. (very rare).

### Get your account details
During a second visit, the bank will give the proxy a copy of the contract, details, connect online banking. You can ask the bank employee to set the desired language for SMS notifications. And what’s more important you will get a contact of the **relationship manager in the bank**.
The proxy will then need to send you the username password to set your phone number on the bank. The person you chose to represent you in front of the bank should then organize all the procedures required for you to receive full control over the bank account you opened.
### Checklist summary
  1. Find the proxy person to whom you will issue a power of attorney (POA)
  2. Draft the text of the POA according to the requirements of the banks.
  3. Notarize and apostille the POA and the passport.
  4. Send the documents by the fast post to Georgia to your proxy.
  5. Wait for the verification of your application
  6. Activate your internet banking and get a complete access to your account
