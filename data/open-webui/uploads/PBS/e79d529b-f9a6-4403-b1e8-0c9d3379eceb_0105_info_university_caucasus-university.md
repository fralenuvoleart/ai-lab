# Caucasus University
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/cu.jpg)
Since its establishment, the Caucasus University has been collaborating with universities around the world, with 207 partners in 61 countries.
The University with its modern infrastructure and equipment is one of the largest higher educational institutions in Georgia and in southern Caucasus.
CU has ten schools: Business, Law, Media, Technology, Architect and Design, Governance, Humanities and Social Sciences, Tourism, Medicine and Healthcare Management, Economics and Education, and provides a high quality, world class education through a combination of well-designed curricula and exceptional faculty with academic credentials and practical experience necessary for delivering an outstanding education.
Since its establishment, the Caucasus University has been collaborating with various universities around the world, with current 207 partners in 61 countries.
