##  Frequently asked questions: International IT Company in Georgia
  * ###  What is international company status in Georgia?
In Georgia, the International Company status is a special tax regime for qualified companies, primarily in the IT and maritime sectors, offering reduced profit and income tax rates of 5% and full tax exemption on dividends.
  * ###  Is Georgia a tech hub?
Yes, with its tech sector boom, major infrastructure projects, and global integration efforts, Georgia is firmly establishing itself as a regional tech hub in the Caucasus.
  * ###  Can a foreigner open a company in Georgia?
Yes, a foreigner can fully open and own a company in Georgia—no local partner, residency, or physical presence is required, and the entire process can be completed remotely.
  * ###  What does it mean international IT company?
An International IT Company in is a tax-registered tech firm, requiring a local office and at least two local employees.
