# Visa-free countries to enter in Georgia
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/residence-passport.jpg)
List of Visa-free countries which are not required a visa to enter in Georgia.
The following countries can enter Georgia visa-free with a max. stay of 1 year (unless otherwise stated).  
| **Region**  | **Country/Territory**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| **EU States**  | All EU member states, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Iceland, Montenegro, Albania, Andorra, Moldova, Serbia, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, Switzerland, Belarus, Turkey  |  
| **Other European Territories**  | Norway, Denmark Territories, French Republic Territories, British Overseas Territories, British Dependent Territories  |  
| **Americas**  | Honduras, Brazil, Canada, Colombia, Panama, Antigua and Barbuda, Costa Rica, Argentina, Uruguay (90 days), Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Bahamas, El Salvador, Mexico, Belize, Saint Vincent and Grenadines, United States of America, Paraguay (90/180 days), Peru (90/180 days), Chile (90/180 days), Barbados  |  
| **Asia and Middle East**  | Armenia, Azerbaijan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Japan, Republic of Korea, Russian Federation, Ukraine (3 years), Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Turkmenistan, Malaysia, Brunei, Thailand, China (30 days – tourism), United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Lebanon, Iran (45 days)  |  
| **Oceania**  | Australia, New Zealand  |  
| **Africa**  | Botswana, Mauritius, Seychelles, South Africa  |  
| **Small States**  | Holy See, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines  |
