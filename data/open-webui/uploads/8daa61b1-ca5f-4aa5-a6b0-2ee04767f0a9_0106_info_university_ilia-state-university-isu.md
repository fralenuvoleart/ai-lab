# Ilia State University (ISU)
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/isu.jpg)
Ilia State University was founded in 2006 as a result of a merger of six different academic institutions with long and varied histories.
Ilia State University was founded in 2006 as a result of a merger of six different academic institutions with long and varied histories. Currently, it is one of the leading research and educational institutions in Georgia, Tbilisi. 
ISU is a multifunctional educational and research institution offering a common space for academic and professional education and research with the joint efforts of its students, professors, teachers and researchers.
