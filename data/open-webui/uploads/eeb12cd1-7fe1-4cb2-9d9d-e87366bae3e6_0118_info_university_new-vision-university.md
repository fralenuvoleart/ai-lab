# New Vision University
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/new-vision-university.jpg)
The New Vision University is a private-level higher educational institution, located in the capital of Georgia, Tbilisi.
The New Vision University is a private-level higher educational institution oriented to provide high-level studies. The university is located in the capital of Georgia, Tbilisi.
The university police are oriented to provide a full package for students’ requirements, such as career servicing, psychological counseling, special needs, etc.
NVU offers western and regional educational systems, prioritizing national and international students. The university has a wide variety of programs, with more than third of faculty being international and becoming a destination of more than 5000 students and alumni from 90 countries.
