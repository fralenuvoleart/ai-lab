##  Frequently asked questions: HR Outsourcing in Georgia
  * ###  What is the controlling authority in Georgia for HR?
The Labor Inspection Office has the authority to inspect, warn, and penalize companies in Georgia if they do not meet the required standards.
  * ###  Do Foreigners Need a Work Visa to Come to Georgia?
The need for a work visa depends on the country of origin. Citizens of countries with which Georgia has visa-free travel agreements do not need a work visa. For all other countries, a D1 visa is required
  * ###  How Do I Legalize My Foreign Employee?
1. Employment Agreement: Start by creating a proper employment agreement.
2. Labor Migration Portal: Report the hiring of a foreign employee to the special labor migration portal.
3. Tax Office: Submit the necessary information to the tax office.
  * ###  Do I Need a Tax Number to Start Work in Georgia?
No, a special tax number is not required to start work in Georgia.
