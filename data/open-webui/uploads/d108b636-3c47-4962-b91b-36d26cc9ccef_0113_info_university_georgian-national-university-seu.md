# Georgian National University (SEU)
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/georgian-national-university.jpg)
Georgian National University SEU is one of the most distinguished universities of higher education with a history of 20 years.
Georgian National University SEU is one of the most distinguished universities in the higher education space of our country with a history of 20 years.
he University aims to create a university environment in line with international standards, where the synthesis and internationalization of strong theoretical, practical and research components in the learning process will ensure the training of competitive professionals for national and international markets.
At present, SEU has 4 faculties and 21 accredited (undergraduate, Master, single-step) educational programs in business, law, social sciences, tourism and medicine, where up to 8000 students study and employ up to 500 academic and visiting staff.
