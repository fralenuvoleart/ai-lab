# Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University (TSU)
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ivane-javakhishvili-tbilisi-state-university.jpg)
Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University is the number one higher educational institution in the Caucasus region, located in Tbilisi.
Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University is number one higher educational institution throughout the Caucasus region, located in Tbilisi, Georgia.
TSU has been ranked among 4% (1,467th out of 35,038 universities in the world) of the best universities in the world, and among 9% (560th out of 6,036 European universities) of the best European universities, according to the Webometrics Ranking of World Universities. TSU is the leader in the Caucasus region.
Today about 22,000 students, including more than 660 foreign nationals, study in TSU’s seven Faculties. TSU’s Faculties include: Exact and Natural Sciences Humanities Social and Political Sciences Economics and Business Law Medicine Psychology and Educational Sciences.
