##  Frequently asked questions: Open a Bank account in Georgia
  * ###  Who can open a bank account in Georgia?
Both Georgian citizens and foreign nationals may open bank accounts in Georgia. However, individuals from countries subject to international sanctions (such as Syria, Iran, and North Korea) may face certain restrictions.
  * ###  How long does it take to open a bank account in Georgia remotely?
The processing time varies depending on the circumstances, but once all required documents are submitted, the bank may need several business days to process the application.
  * ###  How safe is it to deposit funds in Georgian banks?
Georgian banks are stable and reputable financial institutions. The two largest banks—TBC Bank and Bank of Georgia—are both listed on the London Stock Exchange and adhere to stringent international financial regulations. Their financial reports demonstrate strong solvency, making the likelihood of issues arising extremely low.
  * ###  How do I close my account?
In most cases, you can contact your personal bank manager to close your account remotely without needing to visit the bank in person.
  * ###  If I pass away, what will happen to the funds in my account?
The funds will be transferred to your legal heirs upon presentation of valid proof of inheritance.
  * ###  Is a residence permit required to open a bank account?
No residence permit is not required. However, possessing a residence permit can streamline the process and enhance the bank’s confidence in the applicant.
  * ###  Can I open a bank account remotely without visiting Georgia?
Certainly. You only need to authorize a representative in Georgia to handle the account opening procedures.
  * ###  Can I manage my bank account remotely?
Certainly. Banks in Georgia offer advanced internet banking platforms, allowing you to perform all operations through convenient applications. Additionally, personal banking managers can assist you with any transactions via phone or email.
  * ###  Are bank deposits insured?
The deposit coverage amount is relatively low, approximately $6,000 per bank. However, Georgian banks maintain high solvency, and no major bank failures have occurred since the early 1990s.
  * ###  What are the bank fees?
Annual account maintenance fees range from $50 to $100. Common charges include a $15 fee for SWIFT transfers, while ATM withdrawals incur no fees. Please contact us for detailed fee structures specific to individual banks.
  * ###  What currencies can I hold in my Georgian bank account?
Most Georgian banks offer multi-currency accounts, typically supporting the Georgian lari, US dollar, euro, and sometimes the British pound. Other traditional currencies and foreign currencies may also be available depending on your needs.
