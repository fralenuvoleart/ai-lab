# Alte University
![](https://pbservices.ge/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/alte-university.jpg)
Alte University is a fully accredited higher education institution with 20 years of experience, and more than 2,100 students from 17 countries.
Alte University is a fully accredited higher education institution with 20 years of experience and history in the Georgian educational sphere, which unites more than 2,100 students from 17 countries.
University include 5 schools: Business School, International School of Medicine, School of Law and School of Humanities, IT School and Social Sciences. The University currently offers 12 academic programs, both undergraduate and graduate.
Students of Alte University simultaneously study, create and get practical experiences in the spheres they choose.
